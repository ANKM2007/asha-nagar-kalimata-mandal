About the Mandal

Asha Nagar Kalimata Mandal, established in 2007, is a Bengali social, cultural, and religious organisation based in Bhayandar. We are dedicated to worshipping Maa Kali and celebrating our rich Bengali traditions and heritage. Through our annual Kali Maa Puja and cultural activities, we bring people together in faith, unity, and harmony. Our mission is to preserve our traditions, strengthen community bonds, and spread the blessings of Maa Kali. Rani Mayer Joy! 🙏
